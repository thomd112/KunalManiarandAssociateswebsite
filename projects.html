  • Carpe Diem
    +
    Alibaug / Private Residence
    This coastal garden exemplifies xeriscaping in a tropical context. Towering palms interspersed around grass mounds, bold succulents and drought-tolerant natives creare a vibrant, resilient landscapes. Slabs of local grannite are laid loosely on sand, weaving cement-free paths through the estate. Boulders found onsite are strategically littered as interstitial elements, tying the foliage and hardscape into a cohesive whole. The poolscape, with its intimate island and expansive deck offer contemplative spaces to sit and savour dramatic marine vistas.
